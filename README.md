## What this repository is

A static, evidence-first portfolio for recruiters, hiring managers, and technical interviewers. It presents four recurring engineering domains:

- Applied AI / LLM / Agent Systems
- Computer Vision / Video / Edge AI
- Quantitative Research / Trading Systems
- Research / Multimodal & Temporal ML

The site is deliberately **high-signal and low-disclosure**. Public pages show system responsibilities, authority boundaries, evidence, failure modes, research discipline, and ownership. They do not publish private project topology, credentials, proprietary data, strategy parameters, operational runbooks, or client-sensitive implementation details.

## Flagship public case studies

- **Clinical AI & Decision Support** — an EPIC TECHNOLOGY hospital project, presented as a bounded public architecture case study around clinician workflows, AI reasoning, governed evidence, clinical authority, and delivery constraints.
- **Curren** — an independent quantitative research and systems project, presented around point-in-time evidence, research/evaluation discipline, guarded runtime state, and a sanitized public verification boundary. No guaranteed or validated profitable alpha is claimed.

Broader work includes production multi-camera video intelligence, AI-powered education systems, enterprise conversational AI and speech, industrial/edge computer vision, environmental forecasting, and peer-reviewed multimodal/temporal ML research.

## Public abstraction policy

The portfolio should prove engineering depth without becoming an implementation blueprint. Keep public material at the level of stable capabilities and contracts. Never publish:

- patient, clinician, or private corpus data;
- credentials, secrets, tokens, or account state;
- private source/vendor identities where disclosure is unnecessary;
- internal repository/package topology;
- hostnames, machine identities, network layout, or runbooks;
- private model features, strategy parameters, execution controls, or trading databases;
- transient release identifiers, internal branch names, or operator procedures;
- unsupported performance, scale, production, or profitability claims.
